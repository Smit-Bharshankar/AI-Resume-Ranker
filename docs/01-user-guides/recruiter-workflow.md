# Recruiter Workflow

1. Sign in to Sortres.
2. Create a job with a title and full role description.
3. Start AI requirement extraction and wait for its status to complete.
4. Review/edit the structured required skills, preferred skills, experience, and
   mandatory keywords.
5. Activate the job.
6. Open Candidates and upload one or more valid PDF resumes.
7. Wait while Sortres extracts, structures, scores, and generates insights.
8. Review candidates ranked by score; inspect each score breakdown and resume.
9. Update candidate stages as recruiting decisions progress.
10. Retry an eligible failed resume, or delete a test/incorrect item with care.

Scores are decision support. Review the actual resume and requirement breakdown
before taking a consequential hiring action.
