# Personas and Problems

## Primary persona: recruiter or HR professional

**Goal:** identify promising applicants quickly and consistently for an open
role.

**Problems addressed:**

- Large batches of resumes take time to read.
- Keyword-only review can miss relevant candidate evidence.
- Different reviewers can apply inconsistent criteria.
- It is hard to explain why one candidate was prioritized over another.

**Sortres support:** structured job requirements, scored rankings, visible score
breakdowns, source-resume access, AI summaries, interview prompts, and stages.

## Secondary persona: founder or hiring manager

**Goal:** make a shortlist without operating a complex enterprise ATS.

**Sortres support:** an opinionated workflow from job creation through shortlist,
with transparent information rather than opaque automation.

## Non-persona: autonomous decision maker

Sortres must not be framed as a replacement for human hiring judgment. The
platform’s role is organizing evidence and prioritization, not deciding whether
to hire or reject a person.
